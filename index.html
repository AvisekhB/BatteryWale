About us

A neighbourhood battery shop, run like one should be

Battery Wale is a local battery and inverter dealer based in Asansol, West Bengal, supplying genuine batteries for cars, trucks, motorcycles, inverters, UPS systems, gensets and e-rickshaws. We deal directly with manufacturers, which is how we keep prices honest and every unit backed by a real warranty.

Every purchase includes free onsite installation and testing — our technician checks your vehicle or inverter setup on the spot, so you leave with something that actually works, not just something that was sold to you.

If you're planning a new inverter setup and aren't sure what capacity to buy, use our — it's the same math our technicians use in the shop.
